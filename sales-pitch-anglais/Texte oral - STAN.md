# Sales pitch : STAN, le T-Rex (oral d'anglais, environ 5 min)

Méthode FAB : **F**eature (ce que c'est) → **A**dvantage (ce que ça apporte) → **B**enefit (ce que le client y gagne).
Ce texte est fait pour être dit naturellement. Apprends les idées, pas les phrases mot pour mot.

---

**Slide 1 – STAN**
Good morning ladies and gentlemen, and thank you for joining us here at Christie's.
Today, I am not going to sell you a house, a yacht or a car. Anybody can buy those.
Today, I am offering you something that has waited sixty-seven million years for you. Let me introduce Stan.

**Slide 2 – − 67 000 000** *(Feature)*
Stan is a *Tyrannosaurus rex*, the most famous predator that ever walked the Earth.
He lived sixty-seven million years ago, long before the first humans.
And this is not a replica: these are his real, fossilised bones.

**Slide 3 – South Dakota** *(Feature)*
Stan was discovered in 1987 in South Dakota, in the United States, by an amateur palaeontologist called Stan Sacrison. That is where his name comes from.
In 1992, the experts of the Black Hills Institute carefully excavated him.

**Slide 4 – 12 m / 4 m / 188** *(Feature → Advantage)*
Let's talk about size. Stan is twelve metres long and four metres tall.
He has one hundred and eighty-eight original bones, which makes him one of the most complete T-rex skeletons ever found.
In other words, what you see is authentic, and that is extremely rare.

**Slide 5 – 30 000 h** *(Advantage)*
More than thirty thousand hours of work went into excavating, cleaning and mounting Stan.
That means you receive a skeleton that is ready to display on the very first day, with full scientific documentation.

**Slide 6 – 30 cm / ≈ 35 000 N** *(Advantage)*
Look at these jaws. Some of his teeth measure thirty centimetres, and his bite was strong enough to crush a car.
Believe me: wherever Stan stands, he is the star of the room. Every visitor will stop in front of him.

**Slide 7 – 1 / 1 / + ∞** *(Benefit)*
And this is the real value. There is only one Stan in the world. You cannot order another one.
For a museum, Stan means thousands of extra visitors every year.
For a private collector, it means owning a piece of history that nobody else can ever have, a legacy for your family, and incredible prestige.

**Slide 8 – 30 000 000 €** *(Conclusion)*
Stan is offered at thirty million euros.
In 2020, he was sold here in New York for almost thirty-two million dollars, which proves how strong the demand is.
So the only question is: will Stan stay in a storage room, or will he become the centrepiece of your collection?
Thank you very much. I am now happy to answer your questions.

## Vocabulaire utile
- *skeleton* : squelette · *bones* : os · *fossilised* : fossilisé
- *auction house* : maison de ventes aux enchères · *centrepiece* : pièce maîtresse
- *to excavate* : fouiller / extraire · *palaeontologist* : paléontologue
- *one of a kind* : unique · *prestige* : prestige · *legacy* : héritage

## Questions possibles du prof
- *Why should I buy it instead of a villa?* → "A villa can be built again. Stan can't. He's one of a kind."
- *Is it legal?* → "Yes, he was found on private land in the USA, so private ownership is legal."
- *How do you transport it?* → "Our experts deliver and mount it for you."
